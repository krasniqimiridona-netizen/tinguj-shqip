Refund policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Our products are digital files that you can download immediately after payment.

Right of withdrawal

Under EU consumer law, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content delivered immediately, that right ends once the download starts, if you agreed to immediate delivery at checkout and acknowledged that you lose the right of withdrawal.

When we refund

We still want you to be happy with your purchase. Contact us within 14 days of buying and we will refund you or send a working file if:

  • the file is damaged or will not open,
  • you did not receive your download link,
  • you were charged twice for the same product, or
  • you bought the wrong language edition and have not used it (we will swap it for the other edition).

How to ask

Email krasniqimiridona@gmail.com with your order number. Approved refunds are issued through PayPal to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days.
