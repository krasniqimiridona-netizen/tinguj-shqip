Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Shqiponja Digital respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we receive and how we use it, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address and country, which our reseller Lemon Squeezy shares with us so we can deliver your order and support you. We never receive your card details; Lemon Squeezy processes payments under its own privacy policy.
  • When you email us: your email address and the content of your message.

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

Why we use it

To deliver your purchase, answer your questions, and meet our legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your data and do not send you marketing emails unless you ask to receive them.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Support emails are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it, by emailing krasniqimiridona@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec).
